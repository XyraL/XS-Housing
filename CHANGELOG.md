# Changelog

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-10

The first release.

- Houses, apartments, MLOs, offices and motel rooms built in game
- 176 shells and 66 GTA interiors, each place with its own copy
- Buy, rent or take a mortgage, with payments that pay themselves
- Real estate job with listings, tours and commission
- Smart-home panel: lock, lights, music, doorbell, alarm and keys
- Furniture inside and in the yard, with working TVs and speakers
- Break-ins, alarms, cameras and police raids
- Starter apartments for new characters
- Holiday decorations that follow the calendar
- Brings over houses from qb-houses, qbx_properties and ps-housing
